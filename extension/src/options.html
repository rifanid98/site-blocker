Unlock countdown

When on, a blocked page first shows a countdown that looks like the site is about to open. When it reaches zero, the real outcome happens: the block screen or a redirect. Forbidden sites and keywords skip the countdown entirely and shut the PC down at once while shutdown mode is on. Turning it on is free; turning it off needs your password.

The length is fixed while the countdown is on.

Custom blocked page

Replace the built-in block screen with your own. Switch it off any time to go back to the built-in one. Use {{url}} for the blocked address and {{host}} for just the site name.

For safety, scripts, frames, forms, <style> and <link> are removed from your HTML, and only http(s) links and images are kept. Put styling in the CSS box. Pages that block inline images may not show them. Your page is used once you press Save.

Redirect mode

When on, opening a blocked page sends you to the page below instead of showing the block screen. Turning it on is free; turning it off needs your password.

The page you redirect to cannot be on your block list. The address is fixed while the mode is on.

Shutdown mode

When on, opening a page blocked by a forbidden site or keyword shuts this PC down after the countdown. Other blocked pages only show the block screen. Windows shows a warning, and apps with unsaved work get a chance to prompt. Turning it on is free; turning it off needs your password.

Shutting down needs the SiteBlocker Windows app to have been run once, so it can register the link with your browser. To cancel a countdown, run shutdown /a in a terminal. With a countdown of 0 the PC shuts down immediately and there is nothing to cancel.

Backup

Save your block list and settings to a file, or load them from one, for example on another browser. The file does not contain your password. It is plain text, so it lists the sites you block. Exporting is free; importing needs your password.

Merge never removes anything. Your rules win over the file's, and shutdown or redirect mode stay as they are if they are already on. Replace also removes rules, keywords and exceptions that are not in the file, and can switch shutdown or redirect mode off.
